## Schools and tests

**Summary:** I found no change to the maths test format for any of the five profiles. I found a few date and provider items, and I could only confirm some of them from official pages. My searches were limited, so treat "nothing found" as "nothing found", not as proof nothing changed.

**Tiffin Stage 1 and Stage 2**
- **Dates (official):** Tiffin School's 2027-entry Stage One is 2 October 2026. Stage Two was 12 November 2026. 
https://www.tiffinschool.co.uk/admissions/year-7-admissions/

- **Format:** Stage One is English and maths, marked by computer. 
Draft 2026-27 policy: https://www.tiffinschool.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/Admissions-Policy-2026-27-For-Consultation-Dec-2024.pdf
 I found no official announcement changing this.
- **Provider and practice material (official):** Tiffin works with Quest. 
Quest's partnership offers free familiarisation material through Atom Learning for Pupil Premium pupils.
 (https://www.tiffinschool.co.uk/admissions/year-7-admissions/)
- **Provider (unofficial, unconfirmed):** One tutor site says 
Quest now writes the Stage One papers
. It also says 
the test changes each year
. (https://examberrypapers.co.uk/exam-information/11-plus-tiffin-stage-one/) I couldn't confirm when this started.
- **Tiffin Girls':** I couldn't retrieve the school's own page. A third-party page lists Stage One on 1–2 October and Stage Two on 7 November 2026. 
https://www.leadingtuition.co.uk/blog/tiffin-girls-vs-tiffin-school-how-to-choose
 Some sites claim the school uses GL Assessment, which conflicts with the other sources. I'd treat that as unreliable and check the school's page.

**11+ grammar (multiple choice)**
- **Gloucestershire:** All seven grammar schools announced on 15 April 2026 that they are moving from GL Assessment to FSCE. 
It takes effect for September 2028 entry.
 (https://11plusdaily.co.uk/blog/gloucestershire-fsce-change/)
- **Test timing:** The new test moves to Year 5 summer term, and the first sitting is summer 2027. 
https://ggmaths.co.uk/grammar-schools/gloucestershire/

- **Trafford:** One tutor site says Trafford's five schools also join FSCE from 2028. 
https://prep4all.co.uk/fsce-11-plus-exam-guide
 I found no official source for this.
- **CEM:** CEM stopped supplying paper grammar tests in 2023. This is old news, not a new change. 
https://schoolsinsight.co.uk/blog/gl-vs-cem-11-plus.html
