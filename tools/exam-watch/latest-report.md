## Schools and tests

**Summary:** I found no official change to the maths format for Tiffin, the ISEB Pre-Test or 13+ CE. The one confirmed change is the Kent Test provider. Most "who uses what" detail comes from third-party sites. I've marked those as unverified.

**Tiffin Stage 1 and 2 (no format change found)**
- Tiffin School's 2027 entry dates: 
Stage One was 2 October 2026
 and 
Stage Two is 12 November 2026
. [Tiffin School](https://www.tiffinschool.co.uk/admissions/year-7-admissions/)
- Tiffin Girls': 
Stage One was 1–2 October 2026
. [Tiffin Girls'](https://www.tiffingirls.org/admissions/year-7/)
- The boys' school says 
it works with Quest to set its Year 7 entrance tests
, and 
does not publish past papers
. [Tiffin FAQ](https://www.tiffinschool.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/FREQUENTLY-ASKED-QUESTIONS-FAQs-Entry-Sept-2027.pdf)
- The girls' school says 
both stages test English and maths
. [Tiffin Girls' guidance](https://fra1.digitaloceanspaces.com/tiffingirls.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/11120105/web-Summary-guidance-for-Primary-Junior-school-parents-2027.pdf)

**11+ grammar (multiple choice): one confirmed change**
- Kent has 
appointed Quest Assessments as the Kent Test provider from 2027
. It says 
the test's purpose, format, timing and role in admissions stay the same
. [Kent County Council](https://www.kent.gov.uk/education-and-children/schools/school-places/kent-test)
- The council also says 
new familiarisation materials will be available when it has them
. Quest and Kent now host a 
Kent Test 2027 familiarisation site
, which I couldn't check in detail. [Quest](https://www.questassessments.com/parent-guidance-consortium/the-kent-test)
- Other areas (unverified, from tutoring sites): Gloucestershire and Trafford are reported to be moving to FSCE, and Slough and Dorset to Quest. [White Dot Education](https://whitedoteducation.com/insights/11-exam-changes-for-2027-quest-fsce-and-new-test-formats-explained) I found no official council or school pages confirming these.

**ISEB Common Pre-Test (no change found)**

registration runs 9 June 2026 to early May 2027, and testing runs 1 September 2026 to late May 2027
